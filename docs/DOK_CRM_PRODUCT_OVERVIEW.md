## Core Features

### 1. Visual Sales Pipeline (Kanban Board)

See all your deals organized by stage in a drag-and-drop board.

- **6 built-in sales stages:** On Hold → Meeting Pending → Proposal Sent → Negotiated → Verbal Yes → Closed & Won
- Drag a deal card to a new column to instantly move it to the next stage
- Probability auto-updates when a deal moves stages
- Prioritize deals within a stage by reordering cards — order saves automatically
- Each card shows company, solution, revenue, probability, and risk level at a glance

### 2. Smart Dashboard

Your command center from the moment you log in.

- **KPI Cards:** Total Companies, Total Leads, Active Deals, Total Revenue, Weighted Revenue
- **Pipeline by Stage** — bar chart showing deal count per stage
- **Revenue by Solution** — donut chart showing which products are driving revenue
- **Opportunity Heatmap** — bubble chart plotting every deal by probability and value
- **Top Customers** — top 5 companies ranked by pipeline value
- **Recent Activities** — live feed of calls, meetings, emails, notes, and stage changes
- **Revenue Forecasting** — Best Case, Worst Case, Expected This Month, Expected This Quarter
- Fully customizable — show or hide any card or section per user

### 3. Revenue Forecasting

Stop guessing. Know what's coming.

| Forecast | What It Means |
|----------|--------------|
| **Expected This Month** | Weighted revenue from deals ≥ 50% probability |
| **Expected This Quarter** | Full weighted pipeline across all active deals |
| **Best Case** | Total revenue if every active deal closes |
| **Worst Case** | Revenue from high-confidence deals only (≥ 75%) |

### 4. Analytics Page

Deep-dive into your pipeline with interactive charts and filters.

- Filter by Stage and Solution — all charts update instantly
- Sales Funnel chart — see exactly where deals are dropping off
- Deals by Stage, Revenue by Stage, Revenue by Solution
- Opportunity Distribution bubble chart — size = deal value, color = stage
- Stage Analysis Table — deals, revenue, weighted value, and % of pipeline per stage

### 5. Leads Management

Your full lead database, searchable and filterable.

- Search by company name, contact name, or solution
- Filter by sales stage or solution type
- Color-coded risk levels: Green (Low), Yellow (Medium), Red (High)
- Click any lead to open full deal details and edit inline

### 6. Deal Details — Everything in One Place

Every deal card holds the full picture.

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Company Name | The client company |
| Solution | Product/service being offered |
| Contacts | Multiple contacts per deal; one marked as Primary |
| Sales Stage | Current pipeline position |
| Estimated Revenue | Expected deal value (LKR) |
| Probability | 0–100% likelihood to close (slider in 5% steps) |
| Image Count / Box Count | Product-specific quantities |
| Remarks | Internal sales notes |
| H/O Update | Head Office notes |
| Activity Log | Full history of calls, meetings, emails, notes, and stage changes |

### 7. Reports & Export

Generate and share professional reports in seconds.

- **CSV Export** — download all filtered leads as a spreadsheet
- **PDF Export** — branded DOK CRM report with summary stats and full data table
- Filter before export — get exactly the data you need
- Report includes: Total Leads, Total Revenue, Weighted Revenue, Avg Probability

### 8. Sales Targets

Set goals. Track progress. Hit targets.

- Set monthly revenue targets for each sales rep
- Track actual achievement vs target month by month
- View variance — positive (green) or negative (red) at a glance
- Yearly roll-up: total target, total achievement, achievement rate %
- Previous year and next year planning supported

### 9. Admin: Rep Performance Comparison

For sales managers who need the full picture.

- **Company Total Card** — aggregated pipeline, weighted revenue, active deals, and closed won across all reps
- **Per-Rep Cards** — revenue contribution %, accounts, pipeline, weighted revenue, avg deal size, win rate
- **Comparison Charts** — account count and pipeline volume side by side
- **Radar Chart** — 6-dimension normalized comparison: Accounts, Pipeline, Weighted, Avg Deal, Win Rate, Avg Probability
- **Leaderboard** — rank reps by Account Count, Pipeline, Weighted Value, Avg Deal Size, Win Rate
- **Probability Tab** — see where each rep's deals sit across 6 probability buckets
- **Stages Tab** — deal distribution per rep per stage

### 10. Admin: Quarterly Targets

Company-wide target management across all reps and all quarters.

- Yearly summary: total target, total achievement, variance, achievement rate
- Q1–Q4 tabs — each showing target vs achievement per rep
- Side-by-side bar chart comparing target vs achievement per quarter
- Total row showing company-wide figures per quarter

### 11. Team Management

Add, manage, and control your sales team with ease.

- Add new sales reps — set name, username, email, and password
- Activate or deactivate accounts (deactivated users cannot log in)
- Reassign all leads from one rep to another in a single action — for departures or restructuring
- Summary cards: Total Salespeople, Active, Inactive
